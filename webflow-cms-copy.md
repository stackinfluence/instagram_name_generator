## FAQ 1
**Title:** How does the Instagram name generator work?
**Body:** Enter a keyword or your name, choose a content category, and add optional details about your niche. The Instagram name generator then creates ten ideas, each with a handle and a matching display name, formatted to Instagram's username rules so they are ready to use.

## FAQ 2
**Title:** What are Instagram's username rules?
**Body:** An Instagram username can be up to 30 characters and may contain only letters, numbers, periods, and underscores. It cannot start or end with a period or contain two periods in a row, and spaces and other symbols are not allowed. Instagram also lowercases every handle. All of our suggestions already follow these rules.

## FAQ 3
**Title:** What is the difference between a username and a display name?
**Body:** Your username is the unique @handle in your profile URL and in mentions, limited to letters, numbers, periods, and underscores. Your display name is the bold line at the top of your profile and is far more flexible, allowing spaces and mixed case. Our tool gives you both so your profile looks complete.

## FAQ 4
**Title:** Are the generated usernames available?
**Body:** The tool creates ideas, not availability checks, because Instagram has no public username lookup. That is why we add a check link to each handle that opens it on Instagram, so you can confirm whether it is free before you claim it. Popular options may already be taken.

## FAQ 5
**Title:** Can I use this for a business or brand account?
**Body:** Yes. Choose the Business category, or enter your brand name as the keyword, to get handles and display names suited to a company account. If you want the same name on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, check availability on each platform, since their rules differ.

## FAQ 6
**Title:** How many Instagram name ideas can I generate?
**Body:** As many as you like. Each run returns ten fresh ideas, and you can regenerate as often as you want. There is no login, and the tool is free to use.
